# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

OctoAcme uses a consistent, cross-functional approach to delivering product features, services, and integrations. Projects move from **initiation**—validating the problem, success measures, stakeholders, and go/no-go decision—through **planning**, where scope, backlog, milestones, risks, dependencies, and quality expectations become actionable. Delivery is managed through **execution**, including regular standups, delivery syncs, project-board tracking, small pull requests, automated and manual quality checks, and visible progress reporting.

Before production, teams complete **release** readiness activities such as CI and security checks, smoke tests, rollback planning, and stakeholder communication. After delivery or a milestone, teams hold a **retrospective** to capture what worked, identify improvements, assign owners and dates, and feed actions back into the backlog. Across the lifecycle, OctoAcme emphasizes clear role ownership, iterative delivery, prioritized backlogs with acceptance criteria, transparent risk and dependency tracking, regular communication, data-informed decisions, and continuous improvement.

Use these guides as the shared source of truth for project management practices, tailoring them to each project's scale and needs while retaining clear ownership and documented decisions.
